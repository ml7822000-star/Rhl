Protected throughout

  • All reports, drafts, recordings, conversations, approvals and Samsung Contacts are kept exactly as they are.
  • An approved report is never replaced, overwritten or removed automatically.
  • Nothing is ranked by importance, approved, held or discarded unless you do it.
  • The administrative overview is not deleted. It moves below the report.
  • The installed v103 app and the live runner are untouched.
