Case Pilot: Report First

Proposed person screen for your approval. Open a person and the latest approved report is the first thing you read. Calls, recordings and technical details move off the main screen.

Design only · nothing installed

What changes

  • The approved professional report opens immediately. No extra button.
  • One status line at the top: Current or Needs updating, with the approval date and why.
  • The three summary choices sit directly above the report as one tap switch.
  • One clear button: Review new items. The 122 items are sorted into "Likely matters", "Maybe" and "Routine", so you see the important few first.
  • Call history, recordings, IDs and sync details move to Calls & recordings, one tap away.

What stays protected

  • All reports, recordings, conversations and approvals are kept. This is a layout change only.
  • Items waiting for review are never added to the report until you approve them.
  • Samsung Contacts are read and linked as today. Nothing is changed in them.
  • The installed v103 app and the live runner stay untouched until you approve.

Please confirm

  • The three summary choices here are named Brief, Professional and Full. Tell me the exact names v103 uses and I'll match them.
  • Should "Update report" draft a new version for you to approve, keeping the current one live until then? That's what this design assumes.
  • Approve, or tell me what to change.
