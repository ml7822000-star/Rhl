Case Pilot: Report First

Version 2 of the proposed screens. Open a person and the latest approved professional report is the first thing you read. It now uses the three v103 summary names, and updating a report always goes through a draft you approve.

Design only · nothing installed

Changed in version 2

  • The three summary choices use the exact v103 names: One Conversation Only, New Conversations Only, Entire Case Summary.
  • Update report makes a new draft from approved material only. You read it, correct it, and then approve it. Until then the previous approved report stays as it is.
  • The review screen no longer shows importance counts. Items are grouped by date and type. Any AI suggestion is marked as a suggestion only.
  • Nothing is approved, dismissed or hidden automatically. Each item waits for your decision.

What stays protected

  • All reports, recordings, conversations and approvals are kept. This is a layout change only.
  • Approved reports are never replaced, overwritten or removed automatically. Older versions stay available.
  • Items waiting for review never go into a report until you approve them.
  • Samsung Contacts stay linked as they are today. Nothing in them is changed.
  • The installed v103 app and the live runner are untouched.

Before development

  • I haven't seen your v103 screenshots yet. Please upload them in this chat so I can compare this design with your real screens and daily workflow.
  • After that comparison, I'll send you the final screen design to approve. I won't install anything or change the live app before you approve it.
